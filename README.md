## 🚀 Projects

### 📊 [Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis](https://github.com/kishanptll/customer_behaviour_analysis)
End-to-end analysis of 3,900 purchases to uncover spending patterns, subscriber behavior and customer segments.
- Cleaned data in Python (filled 37 missing ratings with category medians, engineered age-group and purchase-frequency features)
- Answered 10 business questions in PostgreSQL (revenue by gender and age group, discount impact, subscriber vs. non-subscriber spending, customer segmentation)
- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard and a business presentation
- **Tools:** Python, Pandas, PostgreSQL, SQL, Power BI

### 📉 [Telecom Customer Churn Analysis & Prediction](https://github.com/kishanptll/telecom-churn-analysis)
Analyzed 6,418 telecom customers to find who is leaving, why, and who is at risk next.
- Cleaned and transformed data in SQL Server, then analyzed churn by tenure, contract, payment method and services
- Built a Random Forest model (Scikit-learn) to predict churn for 411 new customers
- Created a Power BI dashboard combining churn drivers with prediction results
- **Tools:** SQL Server, Python, Scikit-learn, Power BI
